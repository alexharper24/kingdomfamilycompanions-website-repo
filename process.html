How it works

From first hello to go-home day

Bringing home a puppy is a family decision, not an online checkout. Here is exactly what to expect, start to finish.

01

Start an inquiry

Send us a short note about your family, your home, and which breed or puppy caught your eye. There is no fee and no commitment. We read every inquiry and reply personally.

02

Talk with us and meet the puppies

We will set up a call, a video call, or an in-person visit, whichever suits you. We want you to meet the puppies and their parents, and we want to make sure the fit is right in both directions.

03

Reserve your puppy with a deposit

Once we have connected and you have chosen your puppy, a deposit holds them for you.

04

Watch them grow

While your puppy finishes growing up with us, we keep working on socialization and early training, and we send you updates along the way.

05

Go-home day

Pick up your puppy here in northern Indiana, or ask us about safe transportation.

Our puppies leave here knowing what a family sounds like.

Common questions

Questions families ask us

Can we visit before deciding?

Yes, and we encourage it. We welcome families to come and meet our puppies in person. If distance is a problem, we are happy to do a video call instead.

Can you deliver a puppy to us?

Safe transportation can be arranged when needed. Reach out and tell us where you are, and we will talk through the options.

Are the parents health tested?

Yes. Our parent dogs are genetically tested through GenSol and Wisdom Panel and are OFA certified, and every result is linked on our Our Dogs page so you can read it yourself.

How much does a puppy cost?

Our current puppies are $2,200.
