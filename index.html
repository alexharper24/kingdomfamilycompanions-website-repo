How our puppies grow up

Raised in the countryside, ready for the living room

We are blessed to raise our puppies in the peaceful countryside, and we also take them into town for extra socialization and new experiences. By the time a puppy goes home, it has met children, car rides, and the noise of everyday life.

We welcome families to come and meet our puppies in person. We are also happy to set up a video call, and safe transportation can be arranged when needed.

Our Mission

We at Kingdom Family Companion Dogs are committed to breeding high-quality companion dogs that prioritize health, temperament, and family integration. We provide comprehensive information about each puppy’s background and health to ensure a smooth transition into your family.

Why Choose Us

Our dedication to the well-being of our dogs sets us apart in the dog breeding community. Each puppy is raised in a loving environment, ensuring they grow to be well-adjusted and socialized members of your family.

Our Values

We believe every puppy deserves a loving home and a joyful life. Our breeding practices focus on producing not just pets, but cherished family members who will bring love and companionship for years to come.

Health testing you can read for yourself
