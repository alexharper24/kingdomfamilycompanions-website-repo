How our puppies grow up

Raised in the countryside, ready for the living room

We are blessed to raise our puppies in the peaceful countryside, and we also take them into town for extra socialization and new experiences. By the time a puppy goes home, it has met children, car rides, and the noise of everyday life.

We welcome families to come and meet our puppies in person. We are also happy to set up a video call, and safe transportation can be arranged when needed.

Health, in writing

Health testing you can read for yourself

Our parent dogs are genetically tested through GenSol and Wisdom Panel, and OFA certified. We link every result on our site so you can read it before you ever reach out. Each puppy goes home with its health history documented.
