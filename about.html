Our story

A family that has loved dogs for more than twenty years

We are a family who has loved and raised dogs for more than 20 years, and we feel incredibly blessed to raise puppies that can become cherished members of other families.

How we got here

Our journey with Golden Retrievers began in 2024. We quickly fell in love with the breed’s happy-go-lucky personality, gentle nature, and wonderful family temperament. There is something special about a Golden Retriever. They have such a joyful way of bringing people together, and we love watching our puppies grow into confident, loving companions.

In early 2026, we added another beloved breed to our family: the Doberman. The Doberman has been a favorite of mine since childhood. I can still remember picking out a Doberman breed book at a library sale in England when I was a teenager. After all those years, it felt especially meaningful to finally welcome Dobermans into our lives.

My best friend, Joy, helped make that dream possible. Together we co-own Mira, our beautiful Doberman, who lives with Joy and her family. We are excited to continue learning, growing, and raising exceptional dogs while preserving the qualities that make these breeds so special.

What we care about

Temperament, health, and honesty

We breed a small number of litters, and we breed for temperament and health first. Our parent dogs are genetically tested and OFA certified, with every result linked on our Our Dogs page. Each puppy’s health history and lineage are documented, so you know exactly what you are bringing home.
