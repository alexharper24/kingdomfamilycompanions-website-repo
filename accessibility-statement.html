Accessibility Statement

Kingdom Family Companions is committed to making our website usable by everyone, including visitors who use assistive technology. We want every family to be able to read about our dogs and reach us without difficulty.

What we have done

This site is built to meet the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines. Specifically:

  • Text and background colors meet AA contrast requirements.
  • Every meaningful image has descriptive alternative text.
  • Headings follow a clear, logical order on every page.
  • The site can be navigated by keyboard, and focus is visible.
  • Buttons and links are large enough to tap comfortably on a phone.
  • The site respects a reduced-motion preference and stays legible in dark mode.
  • Forms have visible labels tied to their fields.

Where we may fall short

Some content comes from third parties, such as linked health-testing results hosted by GenSol, Wisdom Panel, and the Orthopedic Foundation for Animals. We do not control those pages and cannot guarantee their accessibility.

Telling us about a problem

If any part of this site is difficult for you to use, please tell us and we will fix it. Call or text us at (260) 306-9010 or email info@kingdomfamilycompanions.com, and let us know which page gave you trouble.
