Why this matters

What the testing means for your puppy

Genetic testing screens the parents for inherited conditions common to each breed, and OFA certification evaluates hearts, eyes, hips, and elbows. Testing the parents does not remove every risk, but it removes the preventable ones, and it is the difference between hoping a puppy is healthy and knowing what went into it.
