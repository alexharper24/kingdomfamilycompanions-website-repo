Puppies looking for their families

Our Golden Retriever and Doberman Pinscher puppies are raised here in northern Indiana. We raise them in the peaceful countryside and take them to town for extra socialization and experiences. We welcome families to come and meet our puppies in person, we are available for video calls, and safe transportation can be arranged when needed.
